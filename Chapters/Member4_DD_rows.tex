DD-30 & Check the description against the photos with a fixed rule-based list of English and Roman Urdu phrases (M-04, FR-48). & A language model reads the description; a trained text classifier. & The result is the same every time, can be explained to the seller, costs nothing per lot, and cannot be changed by instructions hidden in the description (SEC-13). No labelled descriptions exist to train a classifier. Accepted limitation: claims in unseen wording are missed, and Urdu script is not handled. \\
DD-31 & Grade a lot by its worst usable photo, and grade it Mixed when its photos include both Good and Major (M-03, FR-46). & Average the photo grades; take the majority grade. & Averaging or voting can hide a major defect that appears in one photo, and a buyer needs to know about it. Mixed sends the lot to review (FR-27). Accepted trade-off: one false detection lowers the grade of the whole lot, and more lots reach the Admin. \\
DD-32 & Classify the material with zero-shot CLIP and include an ``other'' label next to the four materials (M-01, FR-40). & Fine-tune a material classifier; use only the four material labels. & No labelled photo set of surplus materials exists, and zero-shot CLIP needs none. Without ``other'', a photo of something else is forced into the nearest material with a high score. Accepted limitation: zero-shot accuracy is lower on unusual photos, so the prompts and the 0.60 threshold are tuned on the tuning split. \\
DD-33 & Test the price model only on real surplus sale prices, against the depreciation-formula baseline (M-05). & Test on generated prices; test on asking prices from listings. & Generated prices measure the generator rather than the market, and asking prices are not what buyers paid. Accepted limitation: the interviews give only about 25 to 100 real sales, so the result has a wide interval. If the model does not beat the baseline, this is reported, and the baseline formula gives the estimate. \\
DD-34 & Use Claude Sonnet 5.5 (\texttt{claude-sonnet-5-5}) at low effort for the Listing Assistant (M-07). & A larger model such as Claude Opus 5.5; a self-hosted open model. & Answers are short lookups over a few lot fields, so low effort keeps cost and waiting time down (RSK-05). The 4~GB CPU-only AI server cannot host a language model. Accepted trade-off: a cost per question and a dependency on an outside provider, covered by the 20-questions-per-hour limit and the fallback reply (FR-30, FR-56). \\
DD-35 & Audit a random 10\% of auto-published lots offline, as a research procedure (Section~\ref{sec:research-methodology}). & No audit; send every lot to the Admin. & No Admin ever sees an auto-published lot, so without an audit its error rate is unknown. Reviewing every lot would remove the benefit of screening. Accepted limitation: the audit takes team time, and with few lots 10\% gives a wide Wilson interval. It is not an app feature. \\
DD-36 & Train one YOLOv8n defect detector for each material (M-02). & One detector for all materials; larger YOLO models. & Each material has different defect types (Table~\ref{tab:defect-classes}), and the nano size fits the 4~GB CPU-only server with the other models. Accepted trade-off: four models to train, version, and load, and the AGPL-3.0 licence question (P-27). \\
DD-37 & Send a lot with any blurry or dark photo to Changes Requested without an Admin decision, and name the photos to replace (FR-47). & Grade the photos anyway; send the lot to the Admin. & A grade from an unusable photo is unreliable, and asking the seller to retake it saves Admin time. Accepted trade-off: a seller with a poor camera may need several attempts, and the blur and darkness thresholds must be tuned on team photos. \\
DD-38 & Split the team photos by lot and by source, lock the test split, and run the frozen pipeline on it once (Section~\ref{sec:leakage-controls}). & A random split by photo; repeated cross-validation on all photos. & Photos of the same lot or site look alike, so a photo-level split lets the test set leak into tuning and overstates the results. Accepted trade-off: fewer photos remain for tuning, and a result found after the single run cannot be improved and retested on the same split. \\
DD-39 & Give the Listing Assistant no tools and require the structured reply \texttt{\{answer, supported, fieldsUsed\}} (M-07, FR-55). & A free-text reply; let the model query the database. & The API can check which fields the answer used and show the fallback when it is not supported, and a model with no tools cannot act on injected instructions (SEC-10). Accepted limitation: the assistant cannot answer anything outside the lot's data. \\
