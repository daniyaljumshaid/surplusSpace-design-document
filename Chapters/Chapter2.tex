ACD-01 & Assumption & Users have an Android phone or a modern web browser with internet access. & No iOS or desktop application is built. \\
ACD-02 & Constraint & AI assessment covers only four material categories and validated visible defect classes (EXC-01, EXC-02). & The system must disclose its limits and avoid claiming exhaustive inspection. \\
ACD-03 & Dependency & Public defect datasets, published new-material prices, and a small interview/validation sample. & Model coverage and price depreciation estimates depend on data availability and quality. \\
ACD-04 & Constraint & The service launches in one zone, Lahore. & Lots outside an active zone are refused, and the dealer interviews and team photos are collected in Lahore. \\
ACD-05 & Constraint & AI inference runs on a CPU-only server with 4 GB of memory. & Models must be small, lots are screened one at a time, and screening takes seconds rather than milliseconds. \\
ACD-06 & Constraint & The project runs on a six-month schedule (DD-06). & Work runs in parallel tracks with no schedule buffer. \\
ACD-07 & Dependency & An external language-model provider answers Listing Assistant questions. & If it is unavailable, the assistant returns the fallback reply, and the rest of the marketplace works. \\
ACD-08 & Dependency & An email-sending service delivers sign-up verification and password-reset messages. & If it is unavailable, new accounts cannot be verified and passwords cannot be reset. \\
ACD-09 & Dependency & A public map-tile service displays maps for choosing a pickup location. & If it is unavailable, users choose an area from a list instead of a map. \\
ACD-10 & Dependency & Cloud hosting and object storage run the servers and hold lot photos. & Hosting cost must stay within the project budget, and photo access depends on the storage service. \\
ACD-11 & Assumption & Interviewed dealers and contractors will share recent surplus sale prices. & The price model's real-price test set depends on these interviews. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

NFR-25 & Security & Access tokens shall expire after 15 minutes, and every refresh token shall be revoked within 1 minute of sign-out, password change, or suspension. & Must \\

NFR-28 & Security & The server shall reject any lot photo that is not JPEG, PNG, or WebP or that is larger than 2 MB. & Must \\
NFR-29 & Privacy & 100\% of stored lot photos shall have their location metadata removed. & Must \\
NFR-30 & Maintainability & The backend shall run only on runtime versions that receive security updates for the whole project period. & Must \\
NFR-40 & Chat performance & A chat message shall reach an online recipient within 1 second for 95\% of messages, with 50 concurrent users (target). & Must \\
NFR-41 & AI turnaround & AI screening of one lot shall complete within 120 seconds of the worker starting it (target). & Must \\
NFR-42 & Availability & Search, chat, and reservation shall remain usable while the AI Service is down. & Must \\
NFR-43 & Load and concurrency & The REST API and chat hub shall support 50 concurrent users at 10 requests per second (target), checked with k6 or JMeter. & Must \\
NFR-60 & Accessibility & Text on every screen shall have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, or at least 3:1 for large-scale text, meeting WCAG 2.2 Level AA~\citep{wcag22}. & Must \\
NFR-61 & Accessibility & Every interactive control shall have a touch target of at least 48 by 48 logical pixels on Android and Flutter web, exceeding the 24 by 24 CSS pixel minimum of WCAG 2.2 Level AA~\citep{wcag22}. & Should \\
NFR-62 & Accessibility & At a text scale of 200\%, every screen shall keep all of its content and functions, meeting WCAG 2.2 Level AA~\citep{wcag22}. & Must \\
NFR-63 & Accessibility & 100\% of interactive controls and condition-grade icons shall expose a screen-reader label. & Must \\
NFR-64 & Accessibility & On 100\% of screens, no condition grade, lot status, or flag reason shall be conveyed by colour alone~\citep{wcag22}. & Must \\
NFR-65 & Usability & A Contractor shall be able to submit a lot within 3 screens of opening Create Lot. & Should \\
NFR-66 & Usability & In 95\% of interactions on the test device, the system shall show visible feedback within 500 ms of a tap. & Should \\
NFR-67 & Usability & Each lot photo shall be at most 1 MB after on-device compression. & Should \\
NFR-68 & Portability & Screens shall need no horizontal scrolling at widths from 320 to 1920 logical pixels on Android and Flutter web. & Must \\
