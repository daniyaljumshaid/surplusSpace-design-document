DD-01 & Build the Android and web clients from a single Flutter codebase. & Separate native Android and web clients. & One codebase avoids building each screen twice. Layouts must still be tested on both platforms (NFR-68). \\
DD-02 & Use one account that switches between Customer and Contractor profiles. & Separate accounts for buying and selling. & Users keep one sign-in and one history. Screens must make the active profile clear, and Contractor mode still needs Admin approval. \\
DD-03 & Compress lot photos on the device before upload (FR-62). & Upload original photos; compress them on the server. & Smaller uploads suit weak mobile data. Compression could hide fine defects, so the NFR-67 limit needs AI validation. \\
DD-04 & Show the disclosure sentence on every lot details screen (FR-60). & Show it only when reserving; show it only in the terms of use. & It sets correct buyer expectations and avoids a claim the system cannot support (EXC-04). It adds text to a small screen. \\
DD-05 & Show condition grades as an icon and text (FR-67). & Colour-coded badges alone. & Colour must not be the only means of conveying information~\citep{wcag22}. Icon and text take more space on each card. \\
DD-06 & Condense the proposal's 12-month timeline into six months (\tref{tab:schedule}). & Keep the 12-month timeline; shorten it to seven months. & Half of the work must be complete by the end of the semester in December. Parallel tracks leave no schedule buffer. The supervisor has yet to approve the shortened schedule. \\
DD-07 & Leave out the monetization layer. & Keep it as a stretch goal in the final month. & It was the proposal's lowest priority, and the six-month schedule has no time for it. The system has no commission or featured-listing fees. \\
DD-08 & Reassign team roles from the proposal's work division (\tref{tab:team-roles}). & Keep the proposal's role assignment. & The proposal's Table~1.3 lists the four task sets by member number only. Here each set is assigned to a named member, and the task sets themselves are unchanged. Accepted limitation: the proposal's member numbers do not match the role holders. \\
DD-09 & Compare four related systems, leaving out OfferUp. & Compare the proposal's five systems. & OfferUp serves only the United States, so it does not reach the Pakistani buyers and sellers this project targets; the four systems kept include two Pakistani platforms and one surplus-reuse platform. The comparison loses one general resale app. \\
