DD-01 & Build the Android and web clients from a single Flutter codebase. & Separate native Android and web clients. & One codebase avoids building each screen twice. Layouts must still be tested on both platforms (NFR-68). \\
DD-02 & Use one account that switches between Customer and Contractor profiles. & Separate accounts for buying and selling. & Users keep one sign-in and one history. Screens must make the active profile clear, and Contractor mode still needs Admin approval. \\
DD-03 & Compress lot photos on the device before upload (FR-62). & Upload original photos; compress them on the server. & Smaller uploads suit weak mobile data. Compression could hide fine defects, so the NFR-67 limit needs AI validation. \\

DD-40 & Build the backend on ASP.NET Core 10, a long-term-support release. & ASP.NET Core 8, as in the proposal. & .NET 8 stops receiving security fixes on 10 November 2026, during the project, while .NET 10 is supported until November 2028 (NFR-30, RSK-14). Libraries must support .NET 10. \\
DD-41 & Sign in with short-lived access tokens and rotating refresh tokens, Argon2id password hashes, and a required second factor for the Admin. & Server sessions only; long-lived tokens; one shared login rule for every role. & Tokens suit both the Android app and the web client, rotation with reuse detection limits a stolen token, and Argon2id and long passphrases follow current guidance~\citep{owasppasswordstorage,nist80063b4} (NFR-22 to NFR-26). The client must store tokens securely, and the Admin needs an authenticator app. \\
DD-42 & Reserve a whole lot only, with a 48-hour pickup deadline and automatic cancellation 24 hours after it. & Partial quantities; holds with no deadline; cancellation exactly at the deadline. & A whole-lot reservation keeps one AI grade and one Active reservation per lot (NFR-21) and avoids splitting lots; the 24-hour grace lets the seller record a No-Show (FR-16). Buyers who want part of a lot agree in chat and the seller edits the quantity. \\
DD-43 & Show maps with OpenStreetMap tiles, and run distance searches in PostGIS. & A commercial map service such as Google Maps. & No billing account or usage cost, works on Android and the web, and search does not depend on the map provider. Tile usage must show the required attribution (EIR-06). \\
DD-44 & Deliver notifications inside the app: live over the chat hub, and as a stored list. & Mobile push notifications. & Reuses the existing SignalR connection and adds no external push service (FR-76). Users who are not in the app see notifications only when they open it; push is future work. \\
DD-45 & Launch in one zone, Lahore, and accept lots only inside an active zone. & Accept lots anywhere in Pakistan. & Interviews, dealers, and team photos are in Lahore, and a bounded area keeps search results relevant (FR-82). More zones can be added by the Admin later. \\
DD-46 & Let the seller record an optional final sale price when marking a lot sold. & Record nothing; add a separate offer feature. & Real surplus prices are not published, so recorded sale prices give the price model real test data (FR-83, RSK-16). Sellers may leave it empty, so the data will be sparse. \\
DD-47 & Let buyer and seller rate each other after a completed sale, hidden until both rate or 14 days pass. & Buyers rate sellers only; ratings shown immediately. & Both sides gain a trust signal, and hiding ratings until both are in prevents retaliation (FR-80). It adds a second rating per reservation. \\
DD-48 & Verify sellers by an Admin check of business details and a phone call, without identity card numbers or ID images. & Collect national identity card numbers or ID images. & Avoids holding sensitive identity documents (PR-02) while still checking that a seller is real (FR-12). It is a manual check, not legal identity verification (EXC-03). \\
