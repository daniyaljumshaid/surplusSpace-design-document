RSK-01 & AI Service becomes unavailable. & Medium & High & Reduce: keep AI services decoupled, so a lot goes to Pending Review as Not Assessed with no price suggestion (FR-28). & Real-time and AI integration developer \\
RSK-02 & Domain gap: public defect datasets differ from marketplace phone photos, and the prototype gave no detections on a close-up rusty steel photo. & High & High & Reduce: validate on a small team-collected real-photo set before relying on model results. & AI/ML lead \\
RSK-03 & Surplus pricing rests on interviews, because depreciation has no published data. & Medium & Medium & Reduce: ground assumptions in interviews with 5--10 Lahore dealers and contractors, and refine them during evaluation. & AI/ML lead \\
RSK-04 & Public datasets have weak quality or unclear licences: the Hugging Face dataset used in the prototype has no dataset card, so no licence is stated, and NEU's terms still need checking. RSK-05 & LLM cost rises, or the LLM provider is unavailable. & Medium & Medium & Reduce: use Claude Sonnet 5.5 at low effort, limit each user to 20 questions an hour, and return a fallback reply when the LLM provider is unavailable (FR-30). & AI/ML lead \\
RSK-06 & Compute limits slow YOLO fine-tuning. & Medium & Medium & Reduce: use the free Google Colab tier for early work and the budgeted Colab Pro for fine-tuning, and save checkpoints regularly, as Colab limits are not guaranteed. & AI/ML lead \\
RSK-07 & A reservation concurrency fault lets two buyers reserve one lot. & Medium & High & Avoid: use a version-checked update inside a transaction (Figure~\ref{fig:sd-03}) and test it under concurrent load. & Backend developer \\
RSK-08 & AI work starts before the core platform (MS-02) can supply real uploaded photos. & Medium & Medium & Reduce: start AI work in parallel in Month 2 using sample photos; defect detection is already prototyped. & AI/ML lead \\
RSK-09 & Dealers are hard to reach for interviews. & Medium & Medium & Reduce: approach dealers and contractors early, so that 5--10 interviews finish before price assumptions are needed. & AI/ML lead \\
RSK-10 & A team member becomes unavailable. & Medium & High & Reduce: keep all work in the shared Git repository, so another member can continue it. & Real-time and AI integration developer \\
RSK-11 & The 6-month schedule has no buffer. & High & High & Reduce: run tracks in parallel, and scale back material models first if late. & Real-time and AI integration developer \\
RSK-12 & Models do not fit a 4 GB CPU machine. & Medium & High & Reduce: test early and use OpenVINO or ONNX, then move to a larger machine if needed. & AI/ML lead \\
RSK-13 & AWS credit runs out before the demo. & Medium & Low & Reduce: stop idle machines and set a billing alert, then upgrade to the AWS Paid plan and pay from the hosting budget, staying on AWS. & Real-time and AI integration developer \\
RSK-14 & The backend runtime loses security support during the project. & Low & High & Avoid: build on ASP.NET Core 10, a long-term-support release, instead of ASP.NET Core 8, whose support ends on 10 November 2026 (NFR-30). & Backend developer \\
RSK-15 & User accounts are attacked through stolen or guessed passwords. & Medium & High & Reduce: apply NFR-22 to NFR-26 (long passwords, Argon2id hashing, lockout, short-lived tokens, and two-factor Admin sign-in). & Backend developer \\
RSK-16 & Interviews yield too few real sale prices to test the price model. & Medium & Medium & Reduce: ask each dealer for 5--10 recent sales, record final sale prices (FR-83), and report the result honestly against the baseline. & AI/ML lead \\
